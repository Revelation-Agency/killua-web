# Killua Energy: Meta ad copy

Status: staged, not published. Nothing here has been posted, scheduled or sent.

Limits used for every ad: the first line of the primary text stays at 125 characters or fewer, because that is the line that shows before "more". Headlines stay at 40 characters or fewer. Descriptions stay at 30 or fewer. Landing pages are paths only, since the live domain is added when the ads are built.

Service area for location targeting: Fresno, Clovis, Visalia, Merced, Lemoore, Exeter, Los Banos, Manteca and Bakersfield, plus the rest of the Central Valley.

Every ad ends on one of two actions: the short form on the landing page, or a call to (559) 691-4028. The license number to put in front of people is CSLB# 1096633.

## Needs Taj to confirm

- Is the roof inspection, site visit or quote free? Copy says nothing about price, so no ad uses the word.
- Any current offers or discounts. The old agency pages listed "$50 off repairs" and "$1,000 off roof replacement". Neither is confirmed, so neither is used.
- Warranty terms for workmanship, roofing and equipment. None are stated.
- Years in business, review count, star rating, install count, awards. None are used. A real number from Taj would strengthen the trust lines in Ads A and E.
- Financing, lease or PPA options. None are mentioned.
- Equipment brand names for panels, inverters, batteries and chargers. None are named. Confirm whether any may be named.
- Timelines for each install step (permit, install, PG&E permission to operate). No ad gives one.
- Whether Killua handles the permit paperwork (Carousel 1, card 4) and what the company does at the permission-to-operate step.
- Battery backup: whether quotes cover the whole house or selected circuits, and whether every battery quote includes backup. Copy says "keep part of your house running".
- Technical statements to approve: that a battery stores solar power for the evening and can keep part of the house running in an outage (Ad B), and that net billing pays less for exports from new solar customers since April 2023.
- Which solar systems and installers Killua can service, whether a maintenance plan exists, and what a repair visit covers (Ad D, Carousel 2).
- What a roof inspection includes (walking the roof, photos, written findings, attic check) and which roof types Killua installs and repairs (Ad E mentions comp shingle and tile).
- What an EV charger visit covers (panel check, permit, charger types) and whether the license on file covers the electrical work (Ad F).
- License classifications on CSLB# 1096633: confirm they cover solar installation and roofing, plus the electrical work on EV chargers.
- The live domain for the landing pages, and any ad account name or Page handle to tag.
- Rate language is kept general (no PG&E figures, no percentages). A number would need a sourced PG&E figure.
- Real job photos to replace the image placeholders, and permission to show customer homes.
