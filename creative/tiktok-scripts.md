# Killua Energy: TikTok scripts

Status: staged, not posted or scheduled. Five hook-first scripts, each shot on a phone, vertical (9:16), between 15 and 30 seconds. Spoken lines are written to run at a normal talking pace, about 2.5 to 3 words a second, and a spoken phone number takes about 3 to 4 seconds on its own.

Shooting notes for all five:

- Shoot in natural daylight at a real Fresno house. Keep house numbers, license plates, bill account numbers and neighbors' faces out of the frame.
- Whoever is on camera is a real member of the Killua Energy team. No name goes on screen.
- Keep captions in the middle of the frame, away from the bottom and right edges where the TikTok buttons sit.
- Where a script says "real job footage", use clips from an actual Killua job. If none exists yet, shoot the speaker in front of a house instead.
- Default link for every script is `/go/solar-tt`, placed in the bio link. The other option is the phone number, (559) 691-4028.
