# Killua Energy: video scripts and shot lists

Status: staged, nothing generated, posted or scheduled. Three 15-second spots (solar plus battery, solar repair, roofing) and one 30-second brand spot, written to be fed into a Veo-style video generator one shot at a time.

## How to use these prompts

- Each prompt stands alone. The generator does not remember the previous shot, so every prompt repeats the house, the light and the rules.
- Generate a few seconds longer than the shot length listed, then trim in the edit.
- Record the voiceover separately. Add all on-screen text and the end card in the editor. Do not generate letters or numbers in any shot.
- The voiceovers are timed at about 2.8 words a second, and a spoken phone number takes about 3.5 seconds on its own. Read each script aloud against a stopwatch before it goes to a voice actor.
- Paste this into the negative prompt field for every shot: text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, brand names, faces, license plates, crooked panel rows, floating wires.
- Check every clip before it goes in the edit. Panel rows must be straight and evenly spaced. Rails must sit under the panel frames. Conduit must run from the array to a junction box or the wall. Regenerate any clip with a visible face, readable text or a logo.
- Real job footage can replace any generated shot.
- The end card for all four spots reads: Killua Energy, (559) 691-4028, CSLB# 1096633. Keep it on screen for at least 4 seconds.
